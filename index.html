Before you drop it

    How you know it's done

    165°FThickest part of the breast, not touching bone. This is the USDA safe minimum.
    175°FThigh, for dark meat that isn't rubbery. Somewhere in 175–180°F is the sweet spot.
    350°FOil temperature before lowering the bird. Expect it to dip, then hold it at 325–350°F.

    When the fry timer ends, lift the bird out with the burner off and let it rest 20–30 minutes before carving. A rest timer starts from here.
